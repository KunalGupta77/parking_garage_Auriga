Run your parking garage without the guesswork

ParkFlow is a check-in/check-out system for busy multi-level city-centre garages. Attendants assign spots, track EV chargers, and calculate fees correctly — every time.

Key Features

Smart Spot Assignment

Every vehicle is matched to a compatible spot automatically — EVs only ever get an EV charger spot.

Accurate Tiered Billing

First-hour and additional-hour rates with a daily cap, calculated to the minute with no rounding errors.

Instant Plate Lookup

Search any license plate to see active status or full parking history in seconds.

Live EV Availability

See exactly how many EV charging spots are free before you send a driver looking for one.

Multi-Floor, Any Garage

Works for any number of floors and spot mix — not hardcoded to one garage's layout.

Searchable History

Full parking history with search, sorting, and pagination for busy, high-traffic days.

Built for

Parking attendants and facility operators running busy multi-level city-centre garages who need a fast, reliable way to check vehicles in and out, avoid double-booked spots, and bill fairly.

How It Helps

ParkFlow removes manual spot tracking and fee guesswork. Attendants get a single dashboard to check vehicles in, find the right spot instantly, look up any plate, and check out with a fee that's always calculated the same correct way — reducing disputes and idle spot time.
